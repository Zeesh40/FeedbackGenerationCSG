Welcome to the Automated Feedback Generator

This tool helps you generate high-quality, personalized feedback using criteria and percentages — fast, fair, and insightful. No matter the situation, this generator helps you provide consistent, constructive comments in seconds.

Get Started

How It Works

  1. Enter criteria: Provide the name of each assessment criterion — for example, "Clarity"
  2. Assign percentages: Score each criterion with a percentage to reflect performance or weighting.
  3. Add justifications (optional): Enhance the feedback with custom explanations, examples, or context.
  4. Click “Generate”: Instantly receive adaptive, polished feedback paragraphs ready to be used or further customized.

Why It Matters

High-quality feedback boosts learning, but writing it manually takes time. Instead this system ensures consistency, saves time, and empowers educators to focus on what matters most - helping people improve.
